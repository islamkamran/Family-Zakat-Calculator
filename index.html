Understanding the Family Zakat Calculation

1. Purity & Karats (18K – 24K)

24K represents pure gold (99.9%). 22K contains 91.6% gold, 21K contains 87.5%, and 18K contains 75% gold. Jewelers set separate per-tola buying & selling prices for each purity.

2. Resale Deduction (25% Deduction → 75% Resale Value)

When selling gold jewelry back to jewelers, market practice deducts making charges, impurity allowances, and melting margins (standard 25% deduction). Therefore, the actual liquid, cashable value on your Zakat calculation date is 75% of the gross rate.

3. The 40th Part (2.5%) Rule

According to authentic Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), the Zakat on gold and cash is one fortieth (1/40), which equals exactly 2.5%:

Zakat Amount = Net Resale Worth ÷ 40

4. Nisab Threshold

The Nisab for gold is 7.5 Tolas (approx. 87.48 grams). If your family's cumulative gold ownership meets or exceeds this quantity after holding it for one lunar year (Hawl), Zakat is obligatory (Fard).
