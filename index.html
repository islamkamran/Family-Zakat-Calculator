Understanding the Family Zakat Calculation

1. Karats (20K, 21K, 22K)

22K contains 91.6% gold, 21K contains 87.5%, and 20K contains 83.3% pure gold. Jewelers in Pakistan set separate per-tola buying rates for each karat purity.

2. Resale Deduction (25% Deduction → 75% Resale Value)

When selling gold jewelry back to jewelers, market practice deducts making charges, impurity allowances, and melting margins (standard 25% deduction). Therefore, the actual liquid, cashable value on your Zakat calculation date is 75% of the gross market rate.

3. The 40th Part (2.5%) Rule

According to authentic Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), the Zakat on gold and cash is one fortieth (1/40), which equals exactly 2.5%:

Zakat Amount = Net Resale Worth ÷ 40

4. Nisab Threshold

The Nisab for gold is 7.5 Tolas (approx. 87.48 grams). If cumulative family gold ownership meets or exceeds this quantity after holding for one lunar year (Hawl), Zakat is obligatory (Fard).
